Business Logic Reference

  • ◆Active Membership: Direct gym access granted.
  • ◆Grace Period (3 days): Entry allowed for up to 3 days post-expiry.
  • ◆Duplicate Block (60 min): Second check-in within 60 minutes is rejected.
  • ◆Class Auto-Link (15 min): Check-in near a booked class auto-marks attendance.
  • ◆Expired (>3 days): Access denied — renewal required.
